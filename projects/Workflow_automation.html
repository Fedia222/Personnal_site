About Wots AI

Wots AI is a consulting and technical delivery company working across artificial intelligence, machine learning, data and infrastructure. We collaborate with scientific, engineering and business teams to turn a concrete problem into a clearly scoped solution that fits their existing environment.

Our background spans applied mathematics, physics, data science, computer science and high-performance computing (HPC). This foundation informs both our choice of methods and their implementation, from understanding the problem and assessing the available data to designing an architecture and evaluating results.

My Role

As founder and project lead, I help translate scientific and business needs into technical scope, assess possible approaches and define solution architectures. My role combines technical contribution with project coordination, working with client teams to establish priorities, constraints and validation criteria.

Areas of Work

01

AI Integration

Specialised assistants, internal document search, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), model adaptation and task automation. Integrated into existing interfaces or running in the background to support other processes.

02

Data & Machine Learning

Data preparation and quality checks, processing pipelines, exploratory analysis and predictive modelling. Model evaluation and error analysis help identify where existing approaches can improve.

03

Infrastructure & Performance

Compute resource selection, deployment architecture, cost management and performance optimisation. On-premise and private-cloud deployment are a particular focus when data confidentiality and control are central requirements.

04

Scientific Computing & R&D

Support for modelling and numerical simulation projects: analysing simulation outputs, automating numerical experiments, optimising code and computation, and exploring ML methods to approximate or accelerate selected steps.

From Problem to Implementation

  • Understand and scope: Examine the problem, available data, existing tools and operational constraints with the teams involved.
  • Assess feasibility: Compare methods, establish a baseline and define an architecture and measurable validation criteria.
  • Prototype and evaluate: Develop a proof of concept, benchmark candidate approaches and examine errors, performance and resource requirements.
  • Integrate and deploy: Contribute to implementation within existing systems, with deployment choices shaped by data control, maintainability and cost.

Working with Scientific Teams

For R&D projects, we work alongside domain specialists. Our contribution centres on mathematical methods, data, algorithms and computational implementation. The scope is defined after reviewing the scientific question, existing tools and the evidence needed to validate an approach.

Ways to Work Together

An engagement can start with a technical assessment, feasibility study, benchmark or proof of concept. We can also provide focused engineering support to an existing team, with longer-term collaboration developing as the needs become clearer.

The starting point is a real project and a precise contribution: improving model predictions, making better use of compute resources, integrating a private assistant or strengthening an AI and data workflow.
